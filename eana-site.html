About us

We're located on the westside of Atlanta and have a long, rich history!

The purpose of the Association is social and civic: to promote, coordinate and implement all aspects of community planning through neighborhood participation and advice to the Atlanta City Council, the Department of Planning, Development and Neighborhood Conservation and other planning advisory bodies.

To provide a vehicle for maximum citizen participation in promoting the livability and environment of the English Avenue neighborhood. All of which is not for profit (nonprofit).

What this doesn't show you

A permit is a filing, not a finished building. A crime report is what was reported to APD, not a conviction, and reports get reclassified or removed. Numbers alone don't explain who is buying property here, what is being built, or what it means for the people already living on these blocks. That deeper conversation happens in our committee meetings — Public Safety and ZoHoLU dig into the specifics. The general body meeting is where each committee reports that work back to everyone.

For concerns that aren't quite 911 or 311

An unhoused neighbor, someone in a mental health crisis, or a neighbor struggling with substance use isn't a police matter, and it isn't a pothole. That's what PAD (Policing Alternatives & Diversion) is for.

PAD sends a trained response team — not police — to help with immediate needs and connect people to longer-term care. Request PAD the same way you'd request any city service: call 311 and ask for PAD, weekdays.
